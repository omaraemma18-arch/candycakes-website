Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Answers about choosing, ordering, and enjoying a Candycakes bake.

What ingredients do you use?

Ingredients vary by recipe. Our cakes are made with familiar baking ingredients such as flour, eggs, dairy, sugar, and flavourings; each product's description lists its flavour options. Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, dairy, and nuts, so we cannot guarantee that any item is free from allergen traces. Please tell us about allergies before placing an order so we can discuss the recipe with you.

Can I request a custom cake?

Yes. Start with a cake from the menu, then choose from its available flavours and sizes, add an icing message, and describe colours or design ideas in the order notes. If your idea needs a different design or special arrangements, contact us before ordering so we can confirm what is possible.

How much notice should I give?

The product page shows the minimum lead time for each cake. Many cakes need at least three days' notice, and more detailed designs may need longer. Your requested date is confirmed with you by phone after we receive the order.

Do you offer delivery or collection?

Both options may be available depending on your location and the order date. Choose a fulfilment option at checkout to see the available details and any delivery fee. If your address is outside the locations shown, message us before ordering to ask whether delivery can be arranged.

How do I keep my cake fresh?

Keep the cake refrigerated and follow the storage advice we give you when it is handed over. Cakes with fresh fillings or decorations may need particular care, so ask us if you are unsure how to store or serve yours.

How can I ask about an order?

Call or WhatsApp us on +256 709 992 587. For an order already submitted online, keep your phone available so we can confirm the design, date, and fulfilment details.
