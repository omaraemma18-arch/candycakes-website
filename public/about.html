Our story

Made by hand, shared with joy

Candycakes began with a simple idea: the cake at the centre of a celebration should feel as personal as the people gathered around it. What started as a love of baking for family and friends grew into a small bakery creating made-to-order cakes, cupcakes, pastries, and other treats for life's milestones and ordinary happy moments.

A little care in every layer

We make our bakes to order, taking time with the details that turn a cake into someone's cake: the flavour, the size, the colours, and the message written on top. From a familiar vanilla sponge to a rich chocolate celebration cake, our aim is a bake that tastes every bit as good as it looks.

Our kitchen works in small batches so each order gets attention from preparation through finishing. We care about balanced sweetness, tender crumb, smooth fillings, and decorations made thoughtfully rather than rushed.

Our mission

Our mission is to make celebrations warmer through dependable, handcrafted baking and friendly service. We listen to what each customer has in mind, explain the options clearly, and make every effort to create a cake that fits the occasion and the people sharing it.
